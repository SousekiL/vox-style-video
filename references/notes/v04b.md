# v04 Part B: brand anniversary film, f1105–f2234 (44.16 s – 89.32 s)

Work files: `references/analysis/v04/work/partB/` (`panel_diff.csv` = per-frame diff inside the 9:16 panel; `motion.py`, `zoom.py`/`chain.py` = trackers; `measure.py` = colours/halftone; `native/p_NNNNN.png` = native-res panel crops, frame-number aligned and verified against `frames/`).

## 1. Summary
Part B is a **vertical 9:16 edit pillarboxed inside the 16:9 frame** (hard cut at f1110 from the full-frame white paper end card of Part A). It has three registers. (a) A **4-second paper-collage burst** (f1188–f1286): photos cut out of red stock paper, B/W high-contrast halftone, red/pink/green duotones, graph paper, tile-mosaic swaps, RGB-fringed cut-out edges, flickering paper. (b) A repeated **"museum pedestal" formula**: an everyday object on a classical column in a single-colour studio, a slow ease-out dolly push, a small serif caption "This is *not* just a ___." placed *behind* the object, then a 3D transformation into a product, ending on a two-line title card "THIS IS / <PRODUCT>" that cuts on in one frame. (c) A quiet grey-paper end card with a small serif line, then the logo lockup. It is recognisable by the black side bars, saturated one-colour sets (red, magenta, green, blue, orange), white serif caps where the second line is rendered **"broken"/melted**, and hard cuts with no dissolves.

  - The 3D/CG product sections are 0–6 % held (true 25 fps render).

- White is reserved for type and for cut-out highlights. Black appears only as halftone ink and the pillarbox.
- The accents stay in a warm family (red, orange, pink). Green is the only complementary accent, used once (f1274–1286), and it echoes the olive green of Part A.

## 5. Typography
Sizes are % of frame height (720 px, which is also the panel height).

1. **Title, clean line** ("NEVER DONE", "THIS IS"): high-contrast classical roman serif, all caps, regular weight, normal tracking (~0), white #fffff5. Cap height 33 px = **4.6 %** ("THIS IS", f1290). "NEVER DONE" is set so wide that it spans the panel edge to edge (x 2–405) and touches the top edge (cap top at y = 0). Closest Google Font: **Cormorant SC** or **EB Garamond** caps (Trajan-like proportions; **Cinzel** is acceptable).
2. **Title, broken line** ("BREAKING / THE MOLD.", "A VISIONARY", product names): the *same* serif, but the glyphs are **melted/eroded**: swollen bulbous terminals, wobbly stems, bone-like joints (see `work/partB/z_1290_title.png`, `z_1150_title.png`). Cap height ~30 px = 4.2 %. No Google Font matches. Build it as Cormorant SC + SVG `feTurbulence` (baseFrequency 0.04, 2 octaves) → `feDisplacementMap` scale 6 at 720p, plus `feMorphology dilate r=0.8` and a slight blur-threshold (gooey) pass. The concept: line 1 clean, line 2 "broken". Always two lines, centred, at 8–18 % y. Leading: line pitch ≈ 46 px = 6.4 %.
3. **Caption** "This is **not** just a ___.": book serif (Palatino-like), sentence case, regular, white. Ascender-to-baseline 17 px = **2.4 %**, centred at ~51–52 % y, line width ~65 % of the panel. The word **"not"** switches to a **bold grotesk** (pedestal shots) or bold serif (collage) and often gets a **white dry-brush underline/scribble** (rubber band, waffle). In the pedestal shots the caption sits **behind** the object (rubber bands and the snake overlap the letters). Google Fonts: **Crimson Pro** / **Libre Caslon Text** for the sentence, **Inter Bold** or **Archivo Bold** for "not".
4. **Metadata labels** (f1149+): small neo-grotesk, ~1.4 % height, white, left-aligned in a 2-column grid ("Year: 1971 | Story: …", "Dept. of …"), with thin 1 px white rules and an underlined "Department of …" tag next to the TV. Google: **Inter** / **Archivo** regular.
5. **End line**: same book serif at ~1.6 % height, dark grey on grey paper, centred.

Animation of type:
- "NEVER / DONE / BREAKING / THE / MOLD." appears **word by word, one word every 3 frames** (0.12 s): NEVER f1134, DONE f1137, BREAKING f1140, THE f1143, MOLD. f1146. Each word cuts on at full opacity, with no fade, slide or scale.
- Every "THIS IS / X" title **cuts on in a single frame** (both lines together) and stays until the shot cuts: f1287, f1531, f1750, f1927, f2124. Measured: bright-pixel count goes from 0 to full between two frames.
- Captions appear with the shot (cut) and disappear 2–3 frames before the transformation starts (rubber band caption gone at f1664, morph begins f1666).

## 6. Layout and composition
- **Framing device: 9:16 panel in black 16:9 bars.** Everything is composed for the vertical panel. Nothing ever crosses into the bars.
- Pedestal formula: column vertical axis at 50 % x. The object top sits at 50–55 % y, the caption sits directly above or behind the object at 51 % y, and the title band is at 8–18 % y. The camera pushes in until the object fills ~60 % of the panel width.
- Collage layering is **3–4 depth layers**: (1) red paper with creases/grid; (2) rectangular photo cards, hard-edged, no border, no shadow; (3) cut-out objects (shoes) overlapping card corners by 15–25 % of their size; (4) type.
- The collage uses a **tile grid**: a 3×3 grid of image cells with 1 px gutters in the paper colour (f1229–1241), and horizontal 3-band stacks (orange / photo / orange, f1260–1273). The cells are not equal: the centre cell is ~60 % W × 30 % H and the corners are ~45 % W.
- Margins: photo cards inset ~10 % from the panel sides, while shoes and titles deliberately **bleed off the panel edge**.
- Empty space: pedestal shots ~60 % plain studio colour. Collage frames <15 % empty (dense).

## 7. Textures and materials
- **Red stock paper** (#d73d25): flat colour + fine grain (high-pass σ ≈ 4.2 levels at 720p) + **faint horizontal crease/fold lines** every ~1/4 panel height (1 px, 5–8 % darker) + occasional dark vertical photocopy smear (f1188). Code: a `<rect>` fill + noise PNG (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`, opacity 0.15) + 3–4 1-px lines at 8 % black opacity.
- **Paper flicker/boil**: the paper plate swaps texture on irregular holds of 1–3 frames, and its brightness jumps by ±2–3 % (R 213→200 over f1192–1202). Code: choose the noise seed and brightness from `random(Math.floor(frame/holdLen))` with `holdLen` drawn from {1,2,3}.
- **Halftone**: B/W photos are thresholded and screened with a **45° dot/checker screen, pitch 3.35 px at 720p (0.47 % of frame height)**, i.e. ~5 px at 1080p and ~18 px on a 3840-tall canvas. Code: SVG `<pattern>` of circles rotated 45° used as a mask over the luminance, or a fragment shader `step(fract(rot45(uv)*pitch) dist, luma)`.
- **Duotones**: map luminance to two inks (red #5a0d05→#e84b2d, pink #5a2a2a→#f0a090, green #0e1c06→#ffffff). Code: `feColorMatrix type="saturate" values="0"` → `feComponentTransfer` with `tableValues` per channel.
- **RGB fringe on cut-out edges**: cut-out shoes and B/W photos carry a **2–3 px (0.3–0.4 % H) chromatic edge**: magenta/red on the top-left edges, yellow just inside, blue on the bottom edges (`z_1192_shoe.png`, `z_1278_green.png`). Code: render the cut-out three times with `feOffset` dx/dy ±2 px per channel through `feColorMatrix` channel isolation, combined with `feBlend mode="screen"`, or a CSS `drop-shadow(2px -2px 0 #ff00aa) drop-shadow(-2px 2px 0 #0030ff)` on the alpha.
- **Graph paper**: 1 px #ab2306 lines on the red paper, cell ≈ 13 px = 1.8 % H, occupying a rectangular patch (0–50 % x, 6–30 % y), plus **line drawings of shoe soles** (1 px dark red outlines with small square studs).
- **Cut-outs**: hard, slightly aliased edges. **No drop shadows, no white border, no tape** (unlike paper-collage references).
- **Film/video grade over everything in the collage**: soft overall blur (~0.6 px), luma grain, slight vignetting.
- **End card paper**: cool grey #bec0c1 with grain σ≈3.9 and faint mottling.
- Studio CG sections: soft single-key lighting, glossy/foam materials, shallow DOF, motion blur on fast passes. That part is footage, not something to rebuild in code. Use it only as a palette and staging reference.

## 8. Motion vocabulary
1. **Word-stamp title**: each word cuts on at 100 % opacity, one word every **3 frames (0.12 s)**, no easing, left-to-right then line by line. Measured f1134/1137/1140/1143/1146. Stepped, no overshoot.
2. **Single-frame title cut-on**: a two-line title appears complete in 1 frame and holds static (no drift) until the cut. Measured f1287, f1531, f1750, f1927, f2124.
3. **Tile-mosaic swap** (signature collage transition): image A is replaced by image B in **rectangular tiles of random size** (≈ 5–25 % of box width, aligned to a coarse grid). Measured fraction of the photo box showing B: f1209 2 %, f1210 4 %, f1211 15 %, f1212 26 %, f1213 39 %, f1214 62 %, f1215 70 %, f1216 80 %, f1217 86 %, f1218 100 %. **Duration 10 frames (0.40 s), S-curve, 50 % at frame ~5.5** ≈ cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1). Tiles snap on (no fade). Reused at f1240–1247 (grid → worker), f1253–1260 (staircase blocks), f1278–1283 (vertical strips).
4. **Window grow** (duotone square from a point): a red-duotone square opens from a point on the subject's mouth. Width as % of panel: f1222 ~3 %, f1223 ~5 %, f1224 11 %, f1225 30 %, f1226 51 %, f1227 58 %, f1228 59 % (settled). **5 frames (0.2 s) ease-out**, no overshoot. Spring guess: `damping 200, stiffness 300, mass 1` (critically damped) or cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1). A grid of tiles assembles around it on the next 2 frames.
5. **Scatter-out**: the cut-out shoes fly away from the centre as the window grows. Top shoe moves up and out ~22 % H in 4 frames and scales ×1.3. Bottom shoe moves down ~25 % H. Ease-in (accelerating), f1222–1226, exits the panel by f1227.
6. **Stepped boil of collage cards**: card edges jitter ±3–5 px at 720p (0.4–0.7 % H) on irregular holds of 1–5 frames. Photo-card top went y 298→293→290→293, left edge x 49→42→45→39 over f1192–1208. The top shoe drifts 5 px in 10 frames. The paper re-randomises every 1–3 frames.
7. **Ease-out dolly push** (pedestal shots): measured scale vs time.
   - TV f1110→: ×1.135 / 4 f, then 1.095, 1.07, 1.07, 1.06, 1.06, 1.06, 1.045, 1.035, then ~1.01 per 4 f. Cumulative ×1.85 at f1151 (40 f = 1.6 s), ×1.95 at f1175, then hold. The shot **starts at peak speed** (cut in mid-move). Curve ≈ `1 + 0.95*(1-(1-t)^3)` over 65 frames.
   - Bear f1777–1837: ×2.15 over 60 f (2.4 s), steps 1.345→1.105→1.08→1.06→…→1.01 per 6 f, ease-out.
   - Rubber band f1602–1662: ×2.74 over 60 f, nearly constant ~1.15/6 f for 36 f, then ease-out over 18 f.
   - Snake: hold f1357–1369, then ease-in-out ×1.0→1.65 over 36 f (f1369–1405).
   - Waffle iron f1976–2012: slow linear ×1.22 over 36 f (≈0.55 %/frame).
8. **Lunge-to-lens occlusion wipe**: a foreground object (snake head f1496–1500, orange band f1666–1670, shoe f1743–1749) rushes toward the camera, fills >90 % of the panel with motion blur for **4–7 frames**, and the next state is revealed behind it. Smooth 25 fps.
9. **Dip through dark**: the camera pushes into a dark surface (outsole), the frame goes to near-black over 3 frames (f2077–2079), then a hard cut to the bright next shot (f2080).
10. **Shatter-reveal** (CG): the shell breaks into ~40 shards that drift outward and rotate slowly, ~4–8 % of panel per second, over ~40 frames (f1840–1880). For code, approximate with SVG polygons, an outward velocity, and gravity-free rotation.
11. **Tilt-up**: f1437–1451, 15 frames, image moves down ~70 % of the panel height (≈ 117 %/s at peak), ease-in-out.

## 10. Camera
- No virtual camera in the collage (the panel is locked). Motion there comes only from tile swaps, the boil (±0.5 % H) and the shoe scatter.
- Pedestal shots: forward dolly/zoom about the panel centre with no lateral drift (tracker shift ≤2 px). Speeds:
  - TV: initial ~3.4 %/frame of scale (85 %/s), decaying to 0.25 %/frame (6 %/s).
  - Bear: 7.6 %/frame → 0.2 %/frame.
  - Rubber band: ~2.4 %/frame steady, then decay.
  - Waffle iron: 0.55 %/frame (14 %/s).
  - Waffle product shot: slow pull-back −1.6 %/frame for 6 f, then hold.
- Tilt-up f1437–1451 (~117 %/s peak).
- Parallax: the 3D pedestal shots show real parallax (lights and column move faster than the background). No handheld shake. The only "shake" is the stepped boil in the collage.

```tsx
const Panel: React.FC<{children: React.ReactNode}> = ({children}) => {
  const {width, height} = useVideoConfig();          // e.g. 1920×1080
  const pw = Math.round(height * 9 / 16);            // 607.5 → 608
  return (
    <AbsoluteFill style={{background: '#000'}}>
      <div style={{position: 'absolute', left: (width - pw) / 2, top: 0, width: pw, height, overflow: 'hidden'}}>

      </div>

};
```

## 12. Signature details and anti-patterns
Must have:
- The 9:16 panel in black bars, if the deliverable is 16:9. For a native 9:16 deliverable, keep the same composition.
- One flat saturated background hue per shot, and warm reds as the collage base.
- Photos never in natural colour inside the collage, except the hero portrait. Use B/W threshold + 45° halftone or duotones in the paper's own hue.
- Hard-edged rectangular photo cards and cut-out objects with a **2–3 px RGB fringe**, no shadows and no borders.
- Tile-mosaic swaps (10 frames, snap-on tiles) as the collage's main transition, at a very fast beat: a new state every 0.4–0.8 s.
- Two-line serif caps titles: line 1 clean, line 2 "broken/melted". The titles cut on in one frame or word by word on a 3-frame step.
- The caption device "This is **not** just a ___." with a bold "not" and a brush underline, placed behind the object.
- Paper flicker and card boil on irregular 1–5-frame holds, small (≤0.7 % H).

Anti-patterns:
- Crossfades, opacity fades, slide-ins or bouncy overshoot on type. Nothing in Part B overshoots.
- Drop shadows, tape, or torn white paper borders on the cut-outs (this film's collage is flat and print-like).
- More than one saturated background hue per frame (except inside the 3×3 tile grid, which stays within the red/pink family).
- Smooth sub-pixel float on collage elements. Their motion must be held/stepped, while camera pushes stay smooth.
- Titles in sans-serif, or a broken line that is only roughened (it has to look swollen/melted, not just noisy).
- Content leaking into the pillarbox bars.

## 13. Confidence
Measured:
- Panel bounds; the cut frames (in-panel diff); per-section cadence/held ratios.
- Word-reveal frames.
- Title cut-on frames, including the single-frame transitions.
- Mosaic-swap progress curve (pixel fraction).
- Pedestal push-in scale curves (template-matching zoom estimator, ±0.01 per step; parallax adds error on TV/rubber band).
- Halftone pitch and angle (FFT: 3.35 px, 45°).
- All hex values (median of patches on native frames).
- Paper flicker amplitude and hold pattern; card jitter (edge tracking, ±1 px accuracy).
- Title cap heights; caption position and size.

Estimated from viewing frames:
- Red-square widths per frame (read off native strips, ±3 %).
- Shoe scatter distances.
- RGB fringe width (2–3 px from zoomed crops; channel cross-correlation showed no global channel shift, so it is an edge effect, not a whole-frame offset).
- Tile sizes; grid cell size (~13 px).
- The melt filter parameters and the duotone table values are reconstructions, not samples.
- Shatter speed; tilt speed.
- Font matches are nearest equivalents. The broken-serif display face is custom.

Not covered: Part A (f1–f1104). The CG product transformations are rendered 3D footage and are described only as staging and timing references.

# v05: "Kalso | The Story Behind The Original Wellness Shoe" (1GUU)

Measured from `references/analysis/v05/` and native frames extracted to `v05/work/native/` (720×540). Helper scripts are in `v05/work/`: `digits.py` (per-digit diff), `shift.py` (global scroll tracking), `rings2.py` (iris radii), `disc.py`, `echo.py`, `grid.py`. Frame N = time (N-1)/50 s. All px values are native 720×540 unless marked otherwise. % H = % of frame height (540), % W = % of frame width (720).

## 1. Summary
This is a **scroll-driven web "story" page recorded as video**: a brand timeline on flat cream paper. Visually it is closer to editorial web design with a datamosh/echo glitch than to stop-motion paper collage. The opening hook is a **year counter** (1905 → 1957 → 1965 → 1974 → 1980 → 1992 → 2008 → 2021 → back to 1905). Every year is set in a **different display typeface**, and each digit swaps through a staggered **"ghost-smear" glitch** made of semi-transparent copies and sliced strips. After the counter, the page scrolls upward smoothly (ease-in to about 12 px/frame). Old photo prints in aged cream borders, black map silhouettes and monospace captions enter from below. Two recurring devices make it recognisable. The first is the **vinyl-record disc transition**: concentric mauve rings grow behind a year marker and then open a light "label" circle. The second is a constant **vertical echo / frame-stutter glitch**: 1–3 translucent copies of the content offset about 15–25 px vertically, plus 1–3 frame freezes followed by a catch-up jump.

**Rules seen**
- No saturated accent anywhere. The only chroma is one low-saturation **mauve family** (hue ≈ 355°, sat 10–20%) plus whatever the photos contain.
- Two-value world: one paper tone and one near-black ink. Every mid-tone is either a mauve tint of the paper or a translucent copy of the ink (ghosts).
- Background colour changes **only** through the disc transition: cream → mauve → dusty pink. The disc carries the scene colour.
- Photos are always treated (B/W, faded colour or sepia), never clean digital colour.

**Text animation**
- **Digits: per-digit ghost-smear swap** (primitive 2). Each digit animates on its own and digits are staggered L→R. The typeface changes along with the value, so even unchanged digits ("1", "9") glitch when the year's font changes (f20–f24: the "1" glitches for 1905→1957).
- **Caption: line-by-line slice-in** (primitive 4). First a few px-high slice fragments appear (f104: only dots of the descender band show), then the line at ≈ 40% → 80% → 100% opacity over 3 frames. The second line starts 3 frames later and is clipped by a horizontal mask edge (f108 shows only the top half of line 2).
- Exit: text is never animated out on its own. It leaves by page scroll, with echo copies.

## 6. Layout and composition
- **Single centre axis** for the year scenes: logo (31% H), year (centre 49.6% H), caption (66–70% H). Everything is centred at 50% W. Empty paper is ≈ **75–80%** of the frame in the year scenes.
- **Scroll-story column**: content is stacked vertically as on a web page. Each "chapter" is a year marker followed by collage elements below it, and the camera scrolls down the page (content moves up).
- **Collage clusters** (f160–f220, f250–f292): 2 photo cards overlap, the second card overlaps the lower-right corner of the first by about 25% of its area. Cards tilt in opposite directions (−4…−9° and +10…+15°). A black map silhouette sits right of or behind the cards and is partly covered by them. A dashed path (1 px, dash ≈ 4 px, gap ≈ 4 px, grey #8a8780) curves from the caption down to the cards and works as a connector.
- **Depth layers**: (1) paper or disc background → (2) mauve disc rings → (3) map silhouette + its echo copies → (4) B/W card → (5) colour card → (6) text. That makes 5–6 layers. Cards bleed off the frame edges freely.
- **Framing device**: the **disc / record label circle** (outer ring plus inner light circle about 70% H wide) frames each year chapter.
- Margins: the chrome sits about 1–2% from the edges (header at x 6 px, y 20 px; footer ends at x 712, y 525). Collage content ignores the margins and bleeds.

## 8. Motion vocabulary
All smooth at 50 fps unless noted.

1. **Vinyl iris reveal** (f1–f16, end of a disc transition). Concentric bands expand outward from the centre. The innermost paper hole radius as a fraction of the half-diagonal (450 px): f5 0.03, f6 0.07, f8 0.15, f9 0.22, f10 0.38, f12 0.46, f13 0.62, f14 0.71, f15 0.80, f16 1.0. That fits **ease-in t^1.5 over 11 frames (0.22 s)**, roughly `cubic-bezier(0.5,0,0.9,0.6)`. Outer ring at f1 r = 106 px (19.6% H), f2 188, f3 216, then off-frame. Band spacing is ≈ 30–80 px. Each band trails the previous one by 1–2 frames.
2. **Ghost-smear digit swap** (measured on every year, table from `digits.py`). One digit is replaced by 2–4 translucent copies (45% / 18%) of the old and new glyph. The copies are displaced vertically by **±30–60% of the digit height** (≈ 30–60 px). There are also horizontal **strip slices** 8–15 px tall shifted 10–30 px sideways (f22, f23, f31). The copies converge onto the new glyph in its new font.
   - Slow phase (1905→1957, 1957→1965): per-digit active **9–10 frames** (0.18–0.2 s). Digit onsets f20/22/25/29 and f35/38/42/44, so the stagger is **2–4 frames**.
   - Fast phase (1992→2008, 2008→2021): per-digit active **3–4 frames**, stagger **1 frame** (onsets f86/87/88/89).
   - The last digit always settles with a 2–3 frame tail (diff 36 → 17 → 2).
3. **Accelerating counter** (f20–f110). Year onset spacing: 15, 14, 13, 8, 8, 7, then 9 for the return to 1905. Rest time with all four digits clean: 3 f (f17–f19), 1–2 f between mid years, 2 f at 2021 (f92–f93). The final landing on 1905 is a long hold (f109–f118, 10 frames). Timing ≈ ease-in on rate, then a decelerated landing (the last swap stretches to 16 f, f94–f110).
4. **Line slice-in** (captions f104–f110, f234–f242, f256–f262). Per line: frame 0 shows a few horizontal slivers (mask band 3–5 px), frame 1 is the full line at ≈ 40% with a 1-copy vertical echo, frame 2 ≈ 80%, frame 3 is 100%. Line stagger **3 frames**. A horizontal mask edge wipes downward through line 2 in 1 frame (f108). Total 6–7 frames for 2 lines.
5. **Page scroll, ease-in to cruise** (camera, `shift.py`, f118–f292). Cumulative dy: f123 −18, f128 −43, f133 −90, f138 −134, f143 −198, f148 −249, f153 −316, f158 −376. Velocity ramps 0 → 12 px/f over **≈ 25 frames (0.5 s)**, a quadratic ease-in. Cruise is **12–14 px/f** (2.2–2.6% H per frame, **≈ 111–130% H/s**) from f145 to f225, then **≈ 10 px/f** (93% H/s) from f236 to f292. There is no deceleration before the end. Purely vertical: dx = 0 except −4 px total at f174–f181.
6. **Scroll-linked card drift + rotate** (f150–f215). The B/W card rotates **−4° (f152) → −9° (f200)**, about −0.1°/frame. It drifts left ≈ 2 px/f (x of its left edge ≈ 158 → 83 px between f152 and f188). The colour card goes from +10° (f176) to +14° (f200–f212) with x near-constant. Card vertical speed ≈ 11.4 px/f against page 12.5, so a **parallax factor ≈ 0.9**. All linear in scroll position (no easing of its own).
7. **Vertical echo glitch** (text, year, maps, everywhere during scroll). On most moving frames the element is drawn 2–3 times. The main copy is 100%; ghost copies at 45% and 18% are offset **±15–25 px vertically (2.8–4.6% H)** and 0–8 px horizontally, and the offsets change every 1–2 frames (f131 vs f132 vs f133). Clean frames come irregularly about every 3–6 frames (f133, f147, f152, f290).
8. **Freeze-and-jump stutter** (scroll). The page freezes for 1–3 frames, then jumps ahead to catch up, so the average speed is unchanged. Measured dy sequences: f126–f128 (−8, −3, −3), f132–f133 (−21, −1), f148–f149 (−7, −20), f171–f174 (−17, −4, −4, −18), f202–f204 (−22, −4, −4), f228–f230 (−17, 0, −1), f246–f250 (−16, −3, 0, 0, −16), f268–f269 (−3, −23), f272–f273 (−3, −18). That is about **one stutter every 10–25 frames**, each freeze 1–3 frames. It reads as deliberate digital jank and is paired with the echo.
9. **Disc grow + label open** (chapter transition, f206–f248). A mauve disc centred on the incoming year grows from ≈ 330 px diameter (f212, 46% W) → 500 (f220) → 600 (f224) → 700 (f228) → beyond the frame (f232). Its growth speed rises from ≈ 12 to ≈ 25 px/f diameter, so **ease-in over ≈ 30 f (0.6 s)**, while the page keeps scrolling. A pale outer halo ring (#bfb2ad, +15–20 px) leads the disc edge by 1–2 frames. Then the inner light label circle opens from the centre: diameter ≈ 120 px (f236) → 220 (f240) → 330 (f244) → 380 (f248, 70% H). That is an **ease-out over ≈ 12 f (0.24 s)**, ≈ `cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.3,1)`, with no overshoot.
10. **Chrome glitch-out** (f136–f146). The header and footer jump left in 2–3 px steps, show echo copies, and vanish within ≈ 6–10 frames as the scroll starts. No fade curve; it is a cut after the glitch frames.
11. **Dashed path draw** (f113–f121, f198–f210). A 1 px dashed curve appears to grow from its upper end over ≈ 8 frames, then scrolls with the page. Estimated as a `strokeDashoffset`-style reveal.

## 10. Camera
- One continuous **vertical scroll** (web page scroll). Rest f1–f118; ease-in f118–f143; cruise **12–14 px/f** (111–130% H/s) f143–f225; **≈ 10 px/f** (93% H/s) f236–f292. Total travel from f118 to f292 ≈ **1,910 px ≈ 3.5 frame heights**.
- No zoom, no rotation, no shake. Horizontal drift ≈ 0 (−4 px total).
- **Parallax**: photo cards ≈ 0.9× page speed and also rotate (primitive 6). Text, maps and discs move at 1.0×.
- **Stutter** (primitive 8) is the only irregularity. Treat it as a glitch layer, not camera shake.

## 11. Code recipes (Remotion, TSX)
Scale note: values below are written for 1920×1080 (H = 1080). Multiply the source px by 2 (540 → 1080), or use `% H`.

**A. Seeded freeze-and-jump scroll with ease-in**
```tsx
import {useCurrentFrame, interpolate, Easing, random} from 'remotion';
const H = 1080;
export const useScrollY = (start = 118, rampFrames = 25, pxPerFrame = 0.024 * H) => {
  const frame = useCurrentFrame();
  // stutter: every ~10-25 frames freeze 1-3 frames, then catch up
  let f = frame;
  for (let k = start; k < frame; k += 10 + Math.floor(random(`gap${k}`) * 15)) {
    const hold = 1 + Math.floor(random(`hold${k}`) * 3);
    if (frame >= k && frame < k + hold) { f = k; break; }
  }
  const t = Math.max(0, f - start);
  const ramp = Math.min(t, rampFrames);
  // quadratic ease-in to cruise speed, then linear
  return -(pxPerFrame * (ramp * ramp) / (2 * rampFrames) + pxPerFrame * Math.max(0, t - rampFrames));
};
```

**B. Echo layer (vertical ghost copies)**
```tsx
const Echo: React.FC<{children: React.ReactNode; seed: string}> = ({children, seed}) => {
  const frame = useCurrentFrame();
  const bucket = Math.floor(frame / 2);              // offsets change every 2 frames
  const clean = random(`${seed}c${bucket}`) < 0.25;   // ~1 in 4 frames clean
  const off = (i: number) => (random(`${seed}${i}${bucket}`) > 0.5 ? 1 : -1) * (0.028 + random(`${seed}m${i}${bucket}`) * 0.018) * H;
  return (
    <div style={{position: 'relative'}}>
      {!clean && [0.18, 0.45].map((o, i) => (
        <div key={i} style={{position: 'absolute', inset: 0, opacity: o,
          transform: `translate(${(random(`${seed}x${i}${bucket}`) - .5) * 16}px, ${off(i)}px)`}}>{children}</div>
      ))}
      <div style={{position: 'relative'}}>{children}</div>
    </div>
  );
};
```

**C. Ghost-smear digit swap (one digit)**
```tsx
// fonts[]: one family per year, e.g. 'Abril Fatface', 'Archivo Black', 'Shrikhand', ...
const Digit: React.FC<{from: string; to: string; fontFrom: string; fontTo: string; start: number; dur: number; seed: string}> =
({from, to, fontFrom, fontTo, start, dur, seed}) => {
  const frame = useCurrentFrame();
  const p = (frame - start) / dur;                     // dur: 9-10 f slow, 3-4 f fast
  if (p <= 0) return <span style={{fontFamily: fontFrom}}>{from}</span>;
  if (p >= 1) return <span style={{fontFamily: fontTo}}>{to}</span>;
  const h = 0.193 * H;                                 // cap height 19.3% H
  const layers = [0, 1, 2, 3].map((i) => {
    const r = random(`${seed}${i}${frame}`);
    const useNew = r < p;                              // more new-glyph copies as p→1
    const dy = (r - 0.5) * 2 * (0.3 + 0.3 * random(`${seed}d${i}`)) * h * (1 - p);
    const op = i === 0 ? (p > 0.6 ? 1 : 0.45) : (i % 2 ? 0.45 : 0.18);
    return <span key={i} style={{position: i ? 'absolute' : 'relative', left: 0, opacity: op,
      fontFamily: useNew ? fontTo : fontFrom, transform: `translateY(${dy}px)`,
      clipPath: i === 3 ? `inset(${40 + r * 30}% 0 ${10 + r * 20}% 0)` : undefined}}>{useNew ? to : from}</span>;
  });
  return <span style={{position: 'relative', display: 'inline-block'}}>{layers}</span>;
};
// stagger: digit i starts at yearStart + i*stagger, stagger 3 f (slow) → 1 f (fast)
// year onsets @50fps: [20,35,49,62,70,78,85,94]
```

## 12. Signature details and anti-patterns
**Must have**
- A cream paper (#e1decf) and near-black ink (#2f2d2f) two-tone base, with **mauve as the only hue**.
- A **different display typeface per year** in the counter, with the glitch swap staggered L→R, accelerating from ~15 f to ~7 f per year.
- **Translucent ghost copies at two fixed opacities (≈45% / ≈18%)** with vertical offsets for the digit swaps, captions and maps. This is the house glitch.
- **Freeze-and-jump stutter** on an otherwise smooth 50 fps vertical scroll.
- **Concentric disc/record iris** as the chapter transition. Hard-edged flat bands, no gradients.
- Small **monospace captions** with bold keywords, centred under the year (or left-aligned, inverted, on a silhouette).
- Old photos in **thick aged-cream borders** (3.7% H), slightly tilted in opposite directions, with soft low shadows.

**Breaks the look**
- Stepped/on-twos animation or paper boil. This piece is smooth, so don't step it.
- Saturated colours, gradients on the paper, grain/halftone, torn edges, tape or stamps.
- Spring overshoot or bounce. No primitive here overshoots; motion is ease-in, linear or ease-out.
- RGB-split/chromatic glitch. The glitch is monochrome ghosting only.
- Fading whole scenes or cross-dissolving. Changes happen by scroll or disc only.
- Using one font for all years, or a big caption size (keep it ≤ 3% H).

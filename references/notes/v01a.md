# v01 — Part A (f1–f1056, 0.00 s – 35.17 s)

Companion to `v01b.md` (f1033–f2091). Part B already covers the shared grammar: the 12 fps 2:3 cadence, pop-in springs, 3-pose captions, re-roll boil, and the base textures. This file covers what part A **adds or contradicts**, with measurements. Tools and evidence images are in `references/analysis/v01/work/partA/`:
- `track.py`: colour-mask bbox tracker
- `cam.py`: global zoom/shift fit between consecutive changed frames
- `pan.py`: wide horizontal shift search
- `where.py`: changed-region bbox
- `localdisp.py`: block-matching displacement on native frames
- `gridsheet.py`: gridded frame sheets `g_*.jpg`, `t1/t2.jpg`

## 1. Summary
The first half of a paper-collage finance explainer about a bank, houses, a safe, a balloon, a contract, a check, a padlock and a presenter. Greyscale photo cut-outs (bank, houses, hands, man, safe, padlock, money stacks) sit on crumpled-paper, graph-paper and banknote-engraving scans. Each object gets one saturated red-orange accent: the bank door, the safe dial, the toy-house roof, the balloon, the pen. Flat pastel discs (pink grid, pale green) back the hero objects. Part A is recognisable for **whip-pan scene changes**: an accelerating horizontal shove of about 0.6 → 2.5 → 10 % W per step, a hard cut, then the new board decelerating in from the same direction. It also has big-to-small "slam" entries (headline text, question mark), pump-pulse inflation, and a background warp boil every **7–8 frames** (twice as often as in part B).

## 6. Layout and composition
- **Board = sheet patchwork.** 3–5 rectangular paper scans overlap at right angles: crumpled white, graph, kraft, banknote engraving. Vertical seams sit at about 15 %, 45 % and 80 % W. There is always a strip of pure white or green border at one frame edge.
- **Hero zone.** The hero object sits on the centre or a third line, on a pastel disc 35–65 % H in diameter. The disc is often cropped by the frame or a sheet edge.
- **Horizon line.** Shots 3, 7 and 12 use a thin black ruled line (≈ 1 px at 540, y 35–55 %) with a cut-out house row standing on it.
- **Depth.** 5–6 layers: base sheets → pastel disc → texture patches (halftone/plaid) → hero cut-out → hands/tools → tape, sparkles and arrows. No parallax inside a shot (see §10).
- **Margins.** Headlines start at 15 % W / 25–60 % H. They are allowed to be cropped during slam poses.
- **Empty space.** 30–45 % of the frame is plain paper. The balloon-pop hold (f681–693) leaves an almost empty board on purpose.
- **Small marks.** Outline four-point sparkle (≈ 3 % H), "+" crosshair, thin black arrows with an open head, an "×" mark at the top right. Each is 1–2 px at 540.

## 10. Camera
- Same as part B: **no shake, no parallax** inside holds. The `cam.py` fit is ×1.000 and 0 % shift between non-boil steps, e.g. f175–194 and f460–486.
- The board moves only as a rigid layer around cuts:
  - Whip-out: peak about 10 % W per 2–3 frames ≈ **120 % W/s** on the last step.
  - Whip-in settle: first step 23–30 % W in 2–3 frames (≈ 300 % W/s), decaying 0.6× per step.
  - Zoom-out settle: ×1.22 → 1.0 in 18 frames.
- Inside one shot (shot 11) the lock's scale-down and left move (cx 49.9 → 34.1 % W, f886–899) is an object move, not camera: the text and background stay put.

## 11. Code recipes
These assume the 12 fps helpers from v01b (`step12(frame)`). Below, `fs = Math.floor(frame * 12 / 30)` is the animation step and `t = fs * 2.5` is the source-frame time it stands for.

**R1. Split whip-pan transition.** The board ends offset by about 18 % W in the whip direction, and the next board starts about 40 % W behind and decelerates. The first table step (−0.6 % W at k = 0) holds until the whip begins.

```ts
const WHIP_OUT = [0, -0.006, -0.025, -0.10, -0.18]; // fraction of W per step (cumulative), ease-in
const settleIn = (k: number, startOffset = 0.4, r = 0.6) => startOffset * Math.pow(r, k); // k = steps since cut

const outX = (frame: number, cut: number, dir = -1) => {
  const k = Math.floor((frame - (cut - 10)) * 12 / 30); // 4 steps before the cut
  return dir * -WHIP_OUT[Math.max(0, Math.min(k, 4))] * width;
};
const inX = (frame: number, cut: number, dir = -1) => {
  const k = Math.floor((frame - cut) * 12 / 30);
  return -dir * settleIn(k) * width; // starts behind, slides in the dir direction
};
```

## 12. Signature details and anti-patterns
**Must have**
- A whip-pan around every cut: accelerate out in 3–4 steps (×4 per step), hard cut, decelerate in (×0.6 per step).
- One red-orange accent part per board on otherwise greyscale photos.
- Oversized slam entries for headlines and glyph props, with 2 discrete step-downs.
- A 4 Hz warp re-roll on held boards. The board never sits perfectly still for more than 8 frames, but there is zero drift between re-rolls.
- Pastel discs behind heroes (pink grid, pale green), multiplied, often cropped.
- Drop shadows only on flat paper props (check, contract, magnifier), offset down-right with a big soft falloff. Photo cut-outs get none.

**Anti-patterns**
- Cross-fades, blur transitions, or motion blur on whips. Every whip frame is sharp.
- Smooth 30 fps tweening.
- Large overshoot on disc pop-ins in this section (keep it ≤ 3 %; the 20 %+ springs belong to props in part B).
- White sticker outlines around cut-outs.
- Camera shake.
- More than one saturated hue in a board.
- Animating the balloon inflation as one smooth scale. It must pulse with pump strokes.

## 13. Confidence
**Measured**
- Cadence counts.
- Whip/settle shifts (`pan.py`, ±1 px at 540).
- Zoom-settle factors (`cam.py`; exact ±0.002, though moving elements bias it slightly).
- Balloon, house, safe and disc tracks (`track.py` colour masks, ±0.3 %).
- Boil interval and native displacement (`localdisp.py`).
- Halftone pitch and angle (FFT).
- Colour samples.
- Shadow falloff profile.
- Headline cap height and leading.

**Estimated by eye from gridded sheets (±2 % / ±1°)**
- Check rotation and width.
- "?" heights.
- Label wipe states.
- Banknote loop period (≈ 12 f).
- Tentacle wiggle amplitude.

**Guessed**
- The spring and bezier equivalents.
- That P9 is a displacement re-seed rather than a scan swap. Displacement is measured, but a simultaneous grain swap is likely.
- Font matches (Inter is the closest free grotesk; the original may be a commercial neo-grotesk).

The "?" serif face and the brush-strip construction are approximations.

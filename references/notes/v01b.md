## 1. Summary
Part B is the second half of a mixed-media collage explainer: a presenter card with two captions, a hanging house on a rope, a handshake on a "justice" board, a bare tree watered until dollar bills sprout, a brand end card, and a final thumbnail card. Every shot is built from greyscale photo cut-outs (no outline, no drop shadow) laid over scanned paper sheets (crumpled, graph, halftone), with flat semi-transparent colour discs and wedges in pale green, salmon and pink-grid, plus olive-lime tape strips and thin black ink marks. One saturated red-orange accent per scene carries the eye (rope, watering can, slash, logo). What makes it recognisable: **all animation runs on a strict 12 fps grid (2:3 pulldown into 30 fps)**, pop-ins overshoot about 20–28 %, captions jump between 2–3 poses instead of tweening, and the whole background "re-rolls" its paper scan every 15 frames.

## 6. Layout and composition
- **Layer stack (5 depths, back to front):** (1) flat white page → (2) scanned paper sheets (crumpled sheet, graph-paper strip, halftone patch, checked fabric), placed as rectangles that do not fill the frame → (3) flat pastel shapes (disc, half-disc, fan, pie wedge, grid circle) with multiply-like transparency → (4) greyscale photo cut-out hero, always centred on the shape behind it → (5) accents: red line marks, black ink (arrows, X mark, sparkle star, dotted arcs), lime tape strips that overlap edges.
- **Focal placement:** the hero sits on the vertical centre line (x 45–52 %) in 4 of 5 shots. In the tree shot it is a two-subject split (tree x 0–50 %, can x 53–86 %).
- **The shape behind the hero:** every hero has a circle or wedge directly behind it, 1.2–1.6× its size (portrait circle, pink grid circle behind the house and the can, salmon wedge behind the handshake).
- **Edges:** collages bleed off at least one frame edge (paper sheet cut by the bottom, can cut on the right). The white page shows on 1–3 sides, 15–35 % of the area.
- **Overlap rules:** tape strips always straddle a boundary between two layers. Text never sits on a box; it sits directly on paper or photo.
- **Framing devices:** thin black arrow (draw-on), X mark + 12 % W horizontal rule at right (shot 12a, at y 46 %), 4-point sparkle star (about 3 % H), dotted water arcs.

## 10. Camera
- No handheld shake. Global scale and rotation are exactly 1.000 / 0.00° during holds (ORB, 600 inliers).
- Camera moves happen only around cuts:
  - Post-cut settle: pan 25 % W in 12 frames. Average 62 % W/s; first step 12.5 % W in 2 frames, about 190 % W/s peak.
  - Zoom-settle: 0.64 → 1.0 in 15 frames.
  - Mid-shot pan (12b): 34 % W in 12 frames, average 85 % W/s.
  - Pre-cut push: ×1.07 single pose, or ×1.0 → 1.065 → 1.40 over 5 frames (accelerating).
- Parallax: during the f1321 push, the text layer scales ×1.33 while props scale ×1.07, so layers scale at different rates (text nearest). Otherwise the collage moves as one flat board.

## 11. Code recipes (Remotion / TypeScript)

**1. The 12 fps grid (use it for every animated value).**
```ts
import {useCurrentFrame, useVideoConfig} from 'remotion';
const ANIM_FPS = 12;
export const useStepFrame = () => {
  const frame = useCurrentFrame();
  const {fps} = useVideoConfig();             // 30 (or 60)
  const step = Math.floor((frame * ANIM_FPS) / fps); // 2:3 cadence at 30 fps
  return {step, t: (step * fps) / ANIM_FPS};  // t = held frame number to feed interpolate/spring
};
```
At 30 fps this produces holds of 3,2,3,2… and changes on frames where (frame mod 5) ∈ {0, 3} (0-indexed), the same as the source.

**2. Pop-in with overshoot (keyframe table measured from f1219–f1236).**
```ts
const POP = [0.28, 0.81, 1.21, 1.28, 1.17, 1.07, 1.0, 0.98, 1.0]; // one entry per 12fps step
const popScale = (step: number, start: number) => {
  const i = step - start;
  return i < 0 ? 0 : POP[Math.min(i, POP.length - 1)];
};
// stagger: next element starts 4 steps (10 frames) later
// alternative: spring({frame: t - startFrame, fps, config: {mass: 1, stiffness: 130, damping: 8.5}})
```

**3. Cut-and-settle camera + pre-cut push.**
```ts
import {Easing, interpolate} from 'remotion';
const expoOut = Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1);
// first 15 frames of a shot (t from useStepFrame, relative to shot start)

```

## 12. Signature details and anti-patterns
**Must have**
- Everything stepped at 12 fps with 3,2,3,2 holds, including camera moves and springs. Smooth 30/60 fps tweens immediately break the look.
- Overshoot on every entrance of a prop or shape (15–28 %, settled in 15–20 frames). Text does not overshoot; it jumps between poses.
- Paper scans that re-roll every 15 frames while hero photos stay locked.
- Exactly one saturated red-orange element per frame. Everything else is greyscale photos, cool grey paper, flat pastels and small lime strips.
- Hard cuts only, each with a push pose before and an expo-out settle after.
- The white page visible on at least one side. Collages are pasted onto white, not full-bleed.
- Bold neo-grotesk in sentence case, tight tracking, with no boxes behind it.

**Anti-patterns**
- White outlines or drop shadows on the cut-outs (this video has none; only the one stippled photocopy shadow on a paper edge).
- Cross-dissolves, fades, motion blur, or tweened text slides.
- Global camera shake or jitter during holds.
- Several saturated colours at once, or warm cream or sepia paper (the paper is a cool neutral `#e5e4e7`).
- Per-letter typewriter text. Only the marker label reveals progressively, and only in 3 coarse steps.

## 13. Confidence
- **Measured:** 12 fps grid (phase analysis of every change frame); hold histogram; all scale/position series in §8 (colour-mask bbox/area tracker on 540 px frames, about ±0.4 % precision); pendulum amplitude and period (principal axis of the red slash); camera settle and zoom curves (ORB similarity fit, 300–600 inliers); boil cadence and pixel-change area; wedge boil angle; hex colours; type cap height and line spacing; halftone pitch (from a 4× zoom crop, ±0.5 px).
- **Estimated:** spring parameters (fitted to peak timing and overshoot, not to the full curve); the fan-rotate angle (−45°, by eye from the sheets); hand-slide positions (from sheets); bill count and rotation range; the f1564–f1571 lateral pan (ORB results there are noisy, 37–125 inliers, because discs pop at the same time); graph-paper and grid-circle pitch (±20 %); font identification (Inter Tight is a close match, not a confirmed one).
- **Not measured:** audio sync; the f1126–f1206 bird photo crossing behind the caption (seen, not tracked); colour areas are rough visual estimates combined with the stats.json k-means shares for shots 11–15.

# v06 — "Bird & Old Cameras" (12 fps, After Effects)

Source: `references/videos/v06_808818414317782154.mp4`. Work files (helpers, crops, tiles): `references/analysis/v06/work/` (`tile.py`, `track.py`, `cam.py`, `native/` = all 180 frames at 720×1280).
All pixel numbers are at native 720×1280 unless noted. %W = % of frame width (720), %H = % of frame height (1280).

## 1. Summary
A 15 s vertical logo/ident loop on one flat, dusty, saturated red field (#d8332f). Isolated objects (a pure-white bird silhouette with a heavy black wordmark, then three greyscale cut-out photos of vintage cameras) sit dead centre. Each one spins about its vertical axis like a flat card at a constant 15°/frame, and the next object appears at the edge-on moment. 2.5D satellites ride along: a dashed black orbit ring, small letters on a cylinder, a wavy-line plane. Glowing white ring "strobes" flash at the swaps, and 2× scale punches and RGB/slice glitches hit on beats. The animation is drawn on 1s at a deliberately low 12 fps, but the dust/grain plate changes only every 3–4 frames (~3.2 Hz). That split cadence, together with the one-colour red + white + black palette, makes the piece recognisable. It is not paper collage: there are no borders, no shadows and no paper edges. The textural cue is a white-speck film-dust overlay.

Rules:
- **Strict 1 hue.** Red is the only chroma. Everything else is white, black or neutral grey. Photos are fully desaturated and never tinted.
- White = light/positive (bird, rings, text); black = ink (type, dashes, camera body). Never use another accent colour.
- There is no vignette (corner and centre medians are equal, 216/54/49) and no gradient on the background.

## 6. Layout and composition
- **Single centred hero.** Every object is centred on (360, 620–640) = 50%W, 48.5–50%H. Its size is 31–52%W wide and 17–23%H tall, so about 75% of the frame is empty red.
- **Depth layers (2.5D):** (1) background red + dust; (2) the far satellite (dashed ring / wavy-line plane / its red shadow copy); (3) the hero cut-out; (4) the near satellite (letter cylinder, wordmark front copy); (5) ring strobe, drawn screen/add over everything; (6) dust overlay on top of everything (the specks show on the black type).
- **Satellites per object:** bird = 2 wordmark copies + crosshair/X-burst lines. Folding camera = dashed orbit circle (radius ≈140 px = 19%W, stroke ≈10 px, dash ≈28 px, gap ≈14 px) + "W O R X" letter ring. Rangefinder = a plane of ~14 parallel sine-wave white lines (stroke ≈3 px, pitch ≈18 px, plane ≈ 1.3× the camera size) plus a dark-red duplicate on the opposite side. TLR = two vertical letter columns at ±0.3 of camera width outside its edges + 3 discs (white ellipse ≈ 20×35 px).
- **Credits** use a 2-column offset grid: title block left-aligned at 28.9%W starting at 41.8%H; author block right-aligned at 72.9%W at 61.5%H. The bar parks at three anchors: under the title block (208, 677), above the author block (476, 757) and above the title block (208, 490). It never overlaps text.
- Framing devices: the glowing ring (centred on the frame, r 172–489 px = 24–68%W), crosshair and X-burst ticks. No frames, boxes or arrows.

## 8. Motion vocabulary
Every element moves **on 1s at 12 fps** (a new pose every frame). Nothing boils: held text and the bar are pixel-identical frame to frame (bar at (208, 490) on f148–f173, 0 px drift). The "stop-motion" feel comes from the low 12 fps plus the texture plate changing on 3s/4s.

1. **Card spin (Y-rotation turntable)**: the hero is a flat card that rotates about its vertical axis at a **constant ≈15°/frame (180°/s)** from about −75° (edge-on) to +75°. The next object is swapped in at the edge-on frame. Measured from cut-out width = W·cos θ.
   - Folding camera f13–f23: widths 100, 193, 271, 336, 362, 372, 358, 337, 266, 187, 100 → θ ≈ 74, 59, 43, 25, 14, 0, 16, 24, 44, 60, 74°.
   - TLR f35–f45: 57, 116, 159, 193, 216, 223, 215, 193, 162, 108, 57 → 75, 59, 45, 30, 14, 0, 16, 30, 43, 61, 75°.
   - Bird f5–f12: holds front for 3 frames (266, 264, 265), then 256, 225, 182, 124, 57 → 0, 0, 0, 16, 32, 47, 62, 78°.
   - Easing: **linear** (steps of 14–18°; the small variation is measurement noise). Duration 11 frames = 0.917 s per object.
   - The card centre drifts ≈ +36 px (5%W) left → right during the spin (the pivot is slightly behind the card plane), and the height stays within ±5%, so use mild perspective ≈ 1500 px.
   - Satellites share the pivot but sit at different z (dashed ring behind at z ≈ −120 px, letters on a cylinder of radius ≈ 0.7 × card width), so they swing across the card with parallax.
2. **Scale punch (2× hit)**: on a beat, the hero (and its satellites) jumps to **2.0× in 0 frames**, holds 2 frames (frame 1: 1.99×, frame 2: 1.83×), then hard-cuts back to 1× (or away). No easing, no overshoot.
   - Rangefinder f29 → f30 → f31 → f32: w 349 → 693 → 637 → 250 (spin continues underneath).
   - TLR f86 → f87 → f88: h 307 → 619 → 627, then cut to the rings at f89.
   - Bird glitch f48 → f49: 1.97× → 1.0×.
   - Not repeated in the same place on the second pass (punch on the rangefinder in pass 1, on the TLR in pass 2), so treat it as beat-synced rather than part of the loop.
3. **Ring strobe**: a centred white ring that exists for 1 frame per state and jumps radius/thickness every frame instead of animating smoothly. Pattern examples:
   - f89–f92: r 364 / 5 px → 369 / 2 px (faint, peak G 215) → 489 / ≈50 px thick + interior glow → 304 / 6 px.
   - f42: r 172 / 3 px. f44–f45: r 364 / 5 px → 369 / 2 px.
   - Fires at swaps (edge-on frames) and in 1–3-frame bursts every ≈3 frames during the folding-camera beat (f12, f13, f16, f19, f21, f22, f23). The thick ring frames also brighten the whole field (motion diff 58–65).
   - Recipe: pick from a table of [r, stroke, glowOpacity] per frame. Radii cluster at 24%, 42%, 50–51% and 68%W.
4. **Burst-in (crosshair → X → ticks → dots)**, 5 frames, f3–f7:
   - f3: "+" crosshair, 2 lines ≈ 6 px thick through the bird centre, length ≈ 400 px.
   - f4: rotated to 45° ("X"), still touching the centre.
   - f5: X lines start at r ≈ 110 px and end at r ≈ 200 px (gap in the middle).
   - f6: lines collapse to 10–14 px ticks at r ≈ 220 px.
   - f7: 6–8 px square dots at r ≈ 240 px.
   - So: rotation 0 → 45° over 1 frame, then a radial push-out with length shrinking ≈ 50%/frame. Stepped, with no in-betweens.
5. **Glitch-in (slice + double image)**, 2 frames (f1–f2, also f46–f47): horizontal bands displaced, one duplicate of the hero offset ≈ +120 px in x (f2: two birds side by side), letters partially drawn. Then a clean frame.
6. **Scramble-decode text**: 1 frame of random lowercase glyphs (random x ±15 px, y ±12 px, ≈ 0.8 × cap size) per line, then the final string. Lines resolve in order, ≈1 line/frame: f92 line 1, f93 line 2, f94/f95 lines 3–4, f96 line 5 (an oversized 4× glyph flashes), f97 done plus the bar. The name block does the same 4 frames later (f101–f104).
7. **Per-letter type-on with outline-first**: wordmark letters appear one per frame, and the final letter shows stroke-only for 3–4 frames before it fills (f5–f8).
8. **Bar hop (expo ease-out, axis-separated)**: the white bar moves along one axis at a time (L-shaped paths). It moves on every frame, covers ≈46–61% of the distance on the first frame, and loses ≈45–55% of the remaining distance each frame after that. No overshoot.
   - A, x 208 → 476 (268 px = 37%W), f103–f110: 0, .46, .71, ~.83, .91, .97, .99, 1 → 7 frames = 0.58 s.
   - B, y 677 → 757 (80 px), f110–f115: .61, .83, .94, .99, 1 → 5 frames = 0.42 s.
   - Hold 22 frames (1.83 s).
   - C, y 757 → 490 (267 px = 21%H), f136–f141: .51, .76, .90, .98, 1 → 5 frames.
   - D, x 476 → 208, f141–f148: .47, .70, .84, .92, .97, .99, 1 → 7 frames.
   - Fits `Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (easeOutExpo) or a critically damped spring at 12 fps: `spring({fps:12, config:{damping:20, stiffness:170, mass:1}})`. Stagger: B starts on the same frame A ends (0-frame gap); D starts on the frame C ends.
9. **Hard snap-back**: 2× → 1× in 0 frames (f48 → f49). Used to land after a glitch.

## 9. Transitions
- **Edge-on swap (the main one)**, used 7×: frame n shows object A at ≈75° (width ≈ 26% of front width). Frame n+1 shows object B at ≈75° on the opposite side, often with a ring strobe on frame n+1. In detail: f23 (folding camera edge-on, faint ring) → f24 (rangefinder edge-on + wavy plane edge-on). f34 (rangefinder edge-on) → f35 (TLR edge-on + thick ring r 489). f11 → f12 (bird 78° + huge ring flash) → f13 (camera at 74°). It reads like one continuous turntable.
- **Slice glitch cut** (f45 → f49): f45 TLR edge-on + thin ring. f46 full-frame band displacement (grey bands 23%, black 13%, bird at 2× sliced). f47 the same with different offsets. f48 bird 2× with X-burst. f49 bird 1× clean.
- **Ring-strobe bridge to text** (f88 → f93): f88 TLR at 2× punch; f89 empty frame + ring r 364; f90 faint ring r 369; f91 thick ring r 489 + interior glow; f92 ring r 304 + first scramble glyphs; f93 rings gone, decode continues.
- **Block-glitch out** (f173 → f180 → loop): f173 two dark-red blocks (2%); f174 22% dark red; f175–f178 dark red + black + white bars (total 47–66%); f179–f180 mostly black bars (26–33%). It then wraps to f1, whose sparse glitch fragments complete the loop.
- No push, wipe, mask or zoom-through transitions are used.

## 10. Camera
- There is no camera move: no pan, no zoom, no shake (the background texture never translates; the credits' bar/text are pixel-stable).
- All "camera" energy comes from per-object 3D card rotation (180°/s), parallax of the satellites within each object group (dashed ring / letter cylinder swing past the card at different speeds, ≈ 20–40 px offset at front-on), and the 2× scale punches.
- Ring flashes act as exposure flashes: the whole field brightens on thick-ring frames (motion diff 58–68).

## 11. Code recipes (Remotion, composition fps = 12)

**a) 12 fps on 1s + texture on 3s/4s**
```tsx
// Render the comp at fps: 12. If the comp must be 24/30 fps, quantise:
const f12 = Math.floor((frame * 12) / fps);            // animation clock (on 1s @12)
const PLATES = ['dust0.png','dust1.png','dust2.png'];   // + a varying 4th plate
const holds = [4,4,3,4];                                 // 15-frame cycle
const cyc = f12 % 15; let acc = 0, idx = 0;
for (let i = 0; i < 4; i++) { if (cyc < acc + holds[i]) { idx = i; break; } acc += holds[i]; }
const plate = idx < 3 ? PLATES[idx] : `dust_var${Math.floor(f12 / 15) % 6}.png`;
<Img src={staticFile(plate)} style={{position:'absolute', inset:0, mixBlendMode:'screen', opacity:0.85}}/>
```

**b) Card spin with swap at edge-on (linear 15°/frame)**
```tsx
const SEG = 11;                                   // frames per object
const i = Math.floor(f12 / SEG) % objects.length;
const local = f12 % SEG;                          // 0..10
const rotY = interpolate(local, [0, 10], [-75, 75]); // linear, 15°/frame
const drift = interpolate(local, [0, 10], [-18, 18]); // px @720w, pivot behind card
<div style={{perspective: 1500, position:'absolute', inset:0, display:'grid', placeItems:'center'}}>
  <div style={{transformStyle:'preserve-3d', transform:`translateX(${drift}px) rotateY(${rotY}deg) scale(${punch})`}}>
    <div style={{transform:'translateZ(-120px)'}}>{objects[i].farSatellite}</div>
    <Img src={objects[i].src} style={{filter:'grayscale(1) contrast(1.35)'}}/>
    <LetterCylinder radius={0.7*w} letters="VISUALS" />
  </div>
</div>
```
For the bird, hold rotY = 0 for local 0..2 and then use rotY = (local − 2) × 15.

**c) Scale punch (no easing)**
```tsx
const punchAt = [29, 86];            // beat frames (f12 clock, 0-based)

const punch = k === undefined ? 1 : (f12 - k === 0 ? 2.0 : 1.83);
```

**e) Bar hop (axis-separated expo ease-out)**
```tsx
const ease = Easing.bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1);

  interpolate(f12, [a, b], [from, to], {easing: ease, extrapolateLeft:'clamp', extrapolateRight:'clamp'});
const x = f12 < 140 ? seg(102, 109, 208, 476) : seg(140, 147, 476, 208);

```

**f) Scramble decode (1 frame of noise per line)**
```tsx
const rnd = (s: number) => { const v = Math.sin(s * 9301 + 49297) * 233280; return v - Math.floor(v); };
const lineFrame = start + lineIdx;              // ≈1 line per frame
if (f12 < lineFrame) return null;
if (f12 === lineFrame) return <>{[...text].map((_, j) =>
  <span key={j} style={{position:'relative', top:(rnd(j+f12)-.5)*24, left:(rnd(j*7)-.5)*30}}>
```

## 12. Signature details and anti-patterns
Must have:
- One saturated red field (#d8332f) with the white specks/scratches on top of everything, swapping plates every 3–4 frames.
- 12 fps with motion on every frame (not on 2s), against the slower texture cadence.
- A centred, isolated hero that is either pure white or fully greyscale, and that spins as a flat card at a constant 180°/s, with swaps at the edge-on frame.
- 2.5D satellites (dashed circle, letter cylinder, wavy-line plane) sharing the pivot at different depths.
- Glowing white ring flashes that jump in radius and thickness per frame, never tweened.
- Hard 2× punches and slice/block glitches as the only "hits".
- Small, quiet uppercase geometric-sans credits with an expo-eased white bar that moves one axis at a time.

Anti-patterns:
- Tinted or colourised photos, or a second accent colour (no yellow, no blue).
- Drop shadows, white cut-out borders, torn paper, or paper colours other than red.
- Smooth 24/30/60 fps interpolation of the spins or tweened ring growth.
- Easing on the card spin (it must be linear).
- Overshoot or bounce on the bar.
- A vignette or gradient background.
- A moving or shaking camera.
- Busy layouts: the frame should be ≥ 70% empty red.
- Boiling or jittering held text (credits are rock-steady).

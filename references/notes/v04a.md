# v04 — Part A (f1–f1128, 0.00–45.1 s)

Source: `references/analysis/v04/` (1280×720, 25 fps). Range covers sheets `sheet_00001-00024` … `sheet_01105-01128`. Every-frame sheets were read in order. Helper scripts and crops are in `references/analysis/v04/work/partA/` (`cadence.py`, `track.py`, `strip.py`, `sample.py`). Positions are given in % of frame width (x) / height (y) and were measured on the 540×304 frames unless the text says "native".

## 1. Summary
This is a brand-anniversary archive film. It switches between **white-paper editorial pages** and **black "night" pages**. Each scene is a flat collage of archival material: newsprint halftone photos, cream design sheets, taped cards, torn-paper rings and found ads. Over the collage sit **red-orange pencil scribbles, ink-brush marks and hand-lettered notes**. Almost all graphic animation is **stepped on 2s (12.5 fps)**, so it reads as hand-placed stop motion. The recognisable devices are huge cropped serif headlines typed in letter by letter, the logo-shape redrawn in a different medium every 2 frames, physical-paper transitions (unfolding crumpled paper, ink scribble blackout, a slanted paper sheet sliding over), and a **slow ease-in "recede" just before a hard cut**. The middle of the film has a 1-second rapid-fire photo montage that is match-cut on the logo shape.

## 2. Format
- 1280×720, 16:9, 25 fps. Part A is f1–f1128 (45.1 s); the full piece is 89.36 s / 2234 frames.
- Effective animation rate: **12.5 fps (on 2s)** for all collage/graphic scenes. Measured run lengths (diff < 0.3): title f5–120 → {2:46, 3:4, 4:3}; movement f253–346 → {2:41, 3:4}; handwritten collage f487–585 → {2:43, 3:4}; box f661–720 → {2:27, 3:2}.
- Exceptions: the logo-texture cascade f918–980 runs mostly **on 1s** (31 single-frame runs). The quiet white end card f985–1109 is **on 3s** (29 runs of 3): only the paper texture boils. The photo montage f832–917 holds each image 4–7 frames, then 2. Live 9:16 footage from f1110 runs on 1s.
- `stats.json` shot detection is coarse: it finds 16 shots in total, 15 of them in my range. The editorial count in Part A is about 40 distinct set-ups (see shot list).

## 4. Colour
Measured on native frames and keyframes:
- **Paper white** `#fbfbfb`–`#fdfdfd` (std ≈1.5): the main ground, about 45% of Part A screen time. Very faint grey under-sheets `#ececec`.
- **Ink black** `#0e0d0d` (type, logo, brush). **Night black** `#010101` (std 1.1, almost noise-free), about 15% of time.
- **Accent 1, red-orange** `#e74c1d` on white (pencil scribbles, step-repeat logos, hand notes, underline). On black it reads `#bc4621`. The global k-means "deep red" `#aa2a2f` is the darker shade of the found ads/rings. This is the only saturated accent in most frames.
- **Cream sheet** `#ebe9db`–`#f1efdf` (design sheets, taped cards); **chalk white** `#cbcbcb` for handwriting on black.
- **Olive** `#5a6b51` comes from the archive photos (track field, grass). It is not used as a graphic colour in Part A.
- Photo treatment: archive portraits are **greyscale newsprint halftone** (45° screen, pitch 5.3 px at 720p = 0.74% H). Found ads and product shots keep their original colour. Montage clips are untreated full colour.
- Rules: (1) graphic overlays are only black, white and red-orange; (2) one accent hue per frame; (3) colour photos sit on white paper, never on black (the black pages carry B/W photos plus red notes only).
- Area on a typical white page: paper 75–85%, black ink 8–12%, red 3–6%, photo 0–20%.

## 5. Typography
1. **Headline serif** ("NEVER DONE", "MOVEMENT"). A high-contrast transitional/Didone roman in caps with tight tracking (~0). Cap height ≈115 px native = **16% H**, set **bleeding off the top edge** (about 17 px cropped) and spanning >95% of the width. Leading is negative: the gap between lines is 14 px (≈1.9% H). Colour `#0e0d0d`. Animation: **per letter on 2s**: N f19, E f22, V f24, E f26, R f28, D f30, O f32, N f34, E f36 (≈2 frames/char = 12.5 chars/s). Google: **Playfair Display** / **Libre Caslon Display**.
2. **Ink-bleed serif** ("LEAVING A MARK"). The same roman with spread ink and round blob terminals (as if blotted with a dip pen), cap height ≈ 16% H. Appears **word by word**: LEAVING f36, A f40, MARK f44 (4 frames/word). Google: none exact. Use **IM Fell English SC** plus an ink filter (§11 R4).
3. **Night serif** ("WELL, / I DON'T / LOVE IT."). Off-white roman caps, block 37% W, cap height ≈ 7.5% H, with a slightly eroded print edge. Per letter on 2s (WE f428, WEL f430, WELL f432, comma f434, I f436, D f438, O f440, N f442, ' f444, T f447), then LOVE as one word f449 and IT. f455. A red hand underline draws on with LOVE. Google: **Cormorant Garamond SemiBold** caps.
4. **Metadata grotesk** ("Year: / 1971", "Story:: / Swoosh", "Dept. of …"). Neo-grotesk regular, sentence case, cap height 15 px = **2.1% H**, leading 21 px, set in a 2-column label/value grid at the bottom-left. Values type on per character. Google: **Inter** / **Helvetica**-like **Arimo**.
5. **Quirky geometric grotesk** ("AirMax / Tinker", the ad quotes). Medium weight, mixed case, cap height ≈ 6–7% H, has a slanted-bar alternate "e"/"a". Typed per letter on 2s (A f678, Ai M f680, AirM f682, AirMs f684, T f688 …). Google: **Space Grotesk** or **Schibsted Grotesk**.
6. **Small serif names** ("Michael Jordan / Hand Drawn / Swoosh …", "Prefontaine"). A book serif at ≈2.5–3% H, stacked list, each line typed per letter on 2s (about 1 char/2 f). End line "Never done leaving a mark." is ≈2% H at centre (x 33.5–65%, y 47.5–52.6% native), typed N f1002 → … → mark f1022; the second half sits in a lighter weight. Google: **EB Garamond**.
7. **Chalk handwriting** ("Well, I didn't love …"): white `#cbcbcb` brush-pen script, cap height ≈ 4% H, **written on in stroke groups** every 2–4 frames (W f486, Wel f488, Well f490 …). Google: **Reenie Beanie** / **Nanum Pen Script**.
8. **Red hand annotations**: caps engineering lettering (red on black, black on cream), ≈1.5–2% H, appear whole or in 2–3 chunks. Google: **Architects Daughter**.
9. **Stamped/stencil letters** ("D N A" label set next to the logo, giant cream letters f351): geometric sans pieces that hop in one letter per 2 frames.

## 6. Layout and composition
- **White pages** are an editorial grid: huge headline glued to the top edge (0–31% H), hero object in the middle band (40–75%), small 2-column metadata at the bottom-left (x 2–35%, y 76–92%). Margins: left 2%, bottom ~8%. 40–60% of the frame stays empty paper.
- **Black pages** hold one centred cluster about 40–50% wide with 50%+ empty black around it. Text is centred or hangs top/bottom of the photo cluster.
- **Collage pages**: photos overlap at 0° to ±3° rotation. No drop shadows, no outlines. Strips and scraps run off the frame edges, and ephemera are parked along all four edges.
- Depth: 3–4 flat layers (paper ground → sheets/photos → type → red marks). No parallax.
- Framing devices: hand-drawn rectangle with overshooting corners, graph-paper panels, tape strips, red dot stickers, red loops/underlines and arrows, concentric torn rings, a 9:16 pillarbox.
- Overlap rule: red marks always sit on top and may cross type and photos. Type is never boxed.

## 7. Textures and materials
- **Paper ground**: near-white with very faint mottling (std 1–2 levels) that **boils on 3s** in quiet scenes (frame diff ≈1–3/255 every 3rd frame, f985–1109). Code: 3 pre-rendered paper PNGs (or `feTurbulence` seeds) swapped with `seed = Math.floor(frame/3)%3`, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, opacity 0.06.
- **Newsprint halftone**: 45° dot screen with a 5.3 px pitch at 720p (0.74% H), slightly blurred. Code: greyscale photo, then a radial-dot pattern via an SVG `<pattern>` threshold or a GLSL halftone shader.
- **Torn paper rings**: white fibrous torn rims 9–23 px wide at native (1.3–3.2% H) on dark stock. Code: polygon edge with `feTurbulence baseFrequency=0.04 numOctaves=3` + `feDisplacementMap scale=12`, plus a second, larger white path displaced with `scale=20` for the fibre rim.
- **Crumpled foil/paper**: real photographed crumpled sheets (specular grey). Rebuild with an image sequence or a mesh-warp shader; no procedural shortcut looks right.
- **Cream sheets / tracing paper**: `#ebe9db`–`#f1efdf`, no outline, almost no shadow (at most a 1–2 px soft grey edge). Under-sheets are offset 1–3% and rotated 1–2°.
- **Tape**: translucent beige strips (~6% W × 1.5% H) at the card corners and edges, alpha ≈0.7.
- **Graph paper**: thin pale-green grid with ~1.4% W cells.
- **Red pencil**: grainy waxy strokes 3–5 px native with broken texture. Code: SVG path + `feTurbulence` mask (`baseFrequency 0.9`) for grain; **redraw (new seed and jittered control points) every 2 frames**.
- **Ink brush / stipple / hatching variants** of the logo-shape (solid brush, dot stipple, dry-brush hatch, outline, gradient). Prepared as separate SVG/PNG assets and swapped on 2s or 1s.
- No vignette, no light leaks, no film grain on the black pages (std 1.1).

## 8. Motion vocabulary
All moves are stepped on 2s unless noted (`f2 = Math.floor(frame/2)*2`).
1. **Type-on (per letter)**: one glyph every 2 frames (12.5 chars/s), no fade and no slide; occasional 3-frame hiccups. Headline f19–36, night text f428–447, small serif f1002–1022, grotesk f678–720. Long words or a second line pop **word at once** every 4 frames (f36/40/44, LOVE f449).
2. **Hand-write-on**: script appears in stroke groups every 2–4 frames (f486–585, ~4 frames/letter).
3. **Scribble boil**: red pencil strokes are fully redrawn every 2 frames with new curves (f22–118, f192–216). Old strokes vanish and new ones appear, so there is no continuity. Amplitude is the whole stroke layout; keep 3–6 strokes alive.
4. **Medium-swap**: the same logo-shape is replaced every 2 frames by a different rendering (outline → stipple → brush → hatch → solid gradient), scale 60–250% and position jumping ±10% (f72–118). The fast version on 1s runs f943–978.
5. **Zoom-to-sheet (snap push)**: the sheet goes from 31.7% W (f367) → 57.8% (f369) → 73.5% (f372), then settles 73.5 → 75% over f372–380. Scale ×1.82 then ×1.27 then +2%: a strong ease-out over 5 frames (0.2 s). Spring guess: `damping 26, stiffness 260, mass 0.6`, no overshoot. It continues with a slow tilt (centre y 48 → 40.5% over 8 frames = 25% H/s, then stops).
6. **Unfold slide-in** (shoebox f659–677): left edge 77.4 → 72.6 → 64.3 → 57.2 → 53.8 → 51.3 → 51.3 → 48.9 → 48.1 → 43.9% over 18 frames. Ease-out, `cubic-bezier(0.2,0.7,0.3,1)`, while the crumpled leading edge flattens.
7. **Paste-on**: a strip or card appears fully formed (no scale-in), 0° to ±3° rotation, every 2–4 frames (f138–181 newspaper strips; f776–825 posters, every ~4 frames).
8. **Recede-to-cut (signature)**: a slow linear shrink, then an accelerating shrink that ends on a hard cut. Night text width 37.0% (f458) → 35.9% (f476) = −0.06%/frame (~3% scale in 18 f), then 35.0 → 30.8 → 28.9 → 25.0% at f478–484 and a cut at f486. Box scene area 11.2 → 10.4 → 9.5 → 8.1 → 6.8% (scale 1 → .96 → .92 → .85 → .78) at f711–719, cut at f722. Easing: `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` over the last 8 frames, about −25% scale.
9. **Card swap**: a taped card is replaced in place by the next card (hard swap, position jitter ±1%). Holds of 6, 10, 9, 8, 8, 9 frames (f722–771); mean 8.3 f = 0.33 s.
10. **Montage accelerando**: holds 4,4,7,4,4,6,4,7,4,4,6,4,2,5 frames, then nine 2-frame cuts (f897–916). The logo stays near the frame centre (match-cut). Some clips are rotated 5–10° on black (f886–896).
11. **Multiply-and-fly**: the logo duplicates into 3–6 textured copies on 1s; copies scale up 150–400% and exit off the bottom/left within ~12 frames (f949–980).
12. **Echo trail / step-repeat**: a photo or figure duplicated 3–6× with a constant offset (triple-exposure players, photo stacks f505+, the red logo row with fading copies). The copies appear one per 2 frames.
13. **Stepped lateral drift**: the whole MOVEMENT layout drifts right 12.2 → 17.4% over 80 frames (1.6% W/s), in 2-frame steps (f261–341).

## 9. Transitions
- **T1 Unfold reveal** (f119–128): f119 a crumpled ball pokes up at bottom centre (≈8% W). f122 a 30% W crumpled sheet with the photo visible inside. f124 the sheet is ~50% W and the headline is pushed up. f126 ~65% W. f128 full-frame photo, still showing a crumpled rim at the left/right edges. Photo area in the lower 80%: 8.7 → 15.6 → 27.7 → 40.5 → 60% (accelerating, on 2s, 9 frames ≈ 0.36 s).
- **T2 Crumple-away** (f184–197): the collage peels and crumples towards the top right over ~12 frames on 2s, uncovering white paper with the next scene's scribbles already present.
- **T3 Ink scribble blackout** (f409–428): a black brush scribble grows over the sheet. Black coverage 12 → 14 → 22 → 31 → 31 → 44 → 71 → 99% at f413/415/417/419/421/422/424/428. Accelerating, 14 frames; the cut to the black text page happens at 99%.
- **T4 Slanted sheet slide-over** (f586–599): a white sheet with a straight edge ≈15° off vertical (top leans right) jumps in from the right. The mid-height edge sits at 74.4% (f586, held 4 f), 68.9% (f590), 57.4% (f592, held 5 f), then covers to ~10% (f597). The irregular holds read as hand-pushed. Meanwhile the next scene's photo has already been placed on the left side of the black page (f580–586).
- **Hard cuts**: everything else (f4, f347, f355, f367, f428, f722, f826, f832, f917, f1110). There are no dissolves anywhere in Part A.
- **Match cut on shape**: the montage and the logo cascade both keep the logo near the centre across cuts.

## 10. Camera
- No continuous camera on the collage pages. "Camera" moves are layer scales and translations on 2s: snap push (M5), slow drift 1.6% W/s (M13), slow recede −0.06% scale/frame and recede-to-cut (M8), collage zoom-out f649–660 (≈ −15% over 12 f).
- No shake, no parallax, no rotation of the whole frame.
- Live footage (f1110+) has a smooth real push-in on 1s.

## 12. Signature details and anti-patterns
**Must have**:
- 12.5 fps stepping on every graphic layer.
- Huge serif headline cropped by the frame top, paired with tiny grotesk metadata.
- Only black/white/red-orange graphics; red pencil scribbles that redraw every 2 frames.
- Physical paper materials (halftone newsprint, cream sheets, tape, torn rims, crumpled paper).
- Type that appears letter by letter with no fade.
- Hard cuts, with each scene ending on an accelerating recede.
- The logo-shape re-rendered in many media.

**Breaks it**:
- Smooth 25/60 fps tweens on graphics, opacity fades or cross-dissolves.
- Drop shadows or glows; rounded UI cards.
- Extra accent colours, or colour photos on the black pages.
- Centred, polite type that sits fully inside the frame.
- Perfect vector strokes without grain; parallax camera rigs; motion blur.

## 13. Confidence
- **Measured**: cadence histograms per segment; colours (`sample.py`); headline/caption sizes on the native keyframe; halftone pitch (FFT, 5.3 px); torn rim widths; reveal area curve; zoom-to-sheet widths; wipe edge positions and angle; box slide; recede-to-cut widths/areas; blackout coverage; drift rate; card hold lengths; montage hold lengths; type-on frame indices.
- **Estimated or guessed**: spring/bezier constants (fitted by eye to 3–5 stepped samples); font matches; tape/graph-paper sizes; opacity of the echo logos (not measured, my colour probe failed); the crumple-away timing (visual only); scale % in the logo cascade.
- Shot boundaries inside long detected shots come from reading the sheets, not from the detector.

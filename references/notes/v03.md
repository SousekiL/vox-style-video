# v03 "Collage": style notes

Source: `references/videos/v03_632122497727995055.mp4`. Every frame f1–f163 was checked on the 7 every-frame sheets, and every drawing change was opened at native resolution (`work/native/`). Measurement helpers and crops are in `references/analysis/v03/work/` (`lib.py`, `m_drop.py`, `m_road.py`, `m_paper.py`, `m_eye.py`, `m_shift.py`, plus `strip_*.png`, `crop_*.png` and `zoom_*.png`). Coordinates are native px (736×414) unless a value is given as %W or %H.

## 1. Summary
This is a 5.4 s mixed-media collage that builds a single scene layer by layer. It never cuts away. A greyscale photo of a woman walking in a field is covered by a colour Cappadocia-style rock landscape that slides down from the top. A road rises from below. Torn crumpled papers (olive green, then red felt) slide in behind the rocks, and further cut-outs arrive and leave one at a time: an eye, a bird, a ransom-note "TRUST" word, a handwritten note pulled away by a hand, and a hand-held brush that "writes" a script line. The piece ends with a photo of a mouth closing over the whole collage. It is recognisable by four things: (a) every move is stepped at about 10 drawings/s with hard stops and no squash or bounce; (b) the photos are naturalistic but sit in a strict olive, khaki and brown world with one blood-red paper; (c) new elements are inserted between existing depth planes, for example a paper behind the rocks but in front of the sky; (d) a live-action rotoscoped figure walks in place at a fixed anchor point the whole time.

Rules seen in the video:
- **Exactly one saturated colour (red) at a time.** Everything else stays in an olive, khaki or brown range with saturation ≤ 0.45. The cool blue bird is a single tiny exception that is on screen for less than 0.3 s.
- Photos are left in natural colour (not duotoned), but they are chosen to already sit in the khaki range. The intro photo is pure greyscale, and the scene "turns into colour" by slide-over, not by a colour grade.
- Paper colours (cream, olive, red) are flat, matte mid-tones. There is no white anywhere until the teeth arrive.

## 6. Layout and composition
- **One fixed stage, no grid.** The anchor is the walking figure at 45.5%W, feet at 67%H (slightly left of centre, lower third). Every later element is placed around it, and nothing ever covers it until the teeth arrive.
- **Depth layers** (back to front), observed by occlusion:
  1. Cream paper sky.
  2. The olive and red torn papers. They are behind the rocks: rock spires overlap them at f38 and f48.
  3. The rock landscape photo (with its own ground) and the greyscale tree cut-out on the left.
  4. The road.
  5. The figure, the eye and bird, TRUST, the note, the hands and the script.
  6. The mouth photo (frames everything).

  That makes 6 planes. The key rule is that new decorative papers are slipped *behind* the main photo cut-out, so the photo silhouette (the rock spires) stays crisp against them.
- **Corner anchoring.** The large papers come from the top corners (olive top-right, red top-left) and form a V-shaped frame that leaves a cream wedge of sky (3–6%) between them, behind the central spire. Props enter from the edge nearest to where they land: the eye from the top-left corner, the note and hand from the bottom-right, the brush hand from the bottom-left, TRUST and the teeth from the top.
- **Margins.** None. Every large layer bleeds off at least 2 edges. Small props sit 0–15% from an edge.
- **Empty space.** After f48 there is almost none (about 3–6% cream). The composition is dense, but each beat introduces only one new focal prop at a time, and that prop is ≤ 31%H in size.
- **Framing device.** At the end the mouth is a frame: its opening acts as a mask (iris) around the collage.

## 8. Motion vocabulary
All moves are **stepped at the 10 fps grid** (one new pose every 3 frames @30). Each move takes 3–6 drawings and ends with a hard stop. No overshoot was measured on any translation; the only "settle" is the eye's scale.

1. **Slide-over (full-frame layer drop)**: the colour landscape covers the greyscale photo from the top.
   - Measured on f5–f20. Layer translateY in %H: f1–4 −100, f5 −81.2, f8 −64.3, f11 −50.0, f14 −30.4, f18 −16.4, f20 0. Steps of 70, 59, 81, 70 and 68 px.
   - About **16–17%H per drawing**, so 15 frames = 0.5 s at about 162%H/s.
   - Easing **linear**, stepped, hard stop, no overshoot. The layer below does not move.
2. **Rise-in with ease-out**: the road (f23–f29) and the note paper (f89–f98).
   - Road offset from its final y: f23 about +105 px (25%H), f26 +41 px (10%H, scale 0.96), f29 0. That is 3 drawings, 9 frames, 0.3 s.
   - Note paper offset: f89 about +127 px (31%H), f93 +74 (18%H), f95 +24 (6%H), f98 0. That is 4 drawings, 12 frames, 0.4 s. It drifts 2 px horizontally.
   - Step sizes shrink (about 53→50→24 px), which reads as ease-out: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.6, 0.35, 1)` sampled on the grid. No overshoot.
3. **Corner slide-in (torn paper)**: the olive paper from the top-right (f33–f38) and the red paper from the top-left (f41–f48).
   - 3 drawings, 6–8 frames (0.2–0.27 s), starting off-screen at f32 and f40.
   - Olive leading edge on row y=20: x 636→489→364 (147 px, then 125 px = 20%W then 17%W per step).
   - The red texture shifts (0, +63) then (0, +73) px per step, so it is a straight drop of 16–18%H per drawing. Its slanted lower edge is why it reads as diagonal.
   - Roughly linear. The papers start 3 drawings apart (f33 and f41), so they overlap by 1 drawing (0.27 s stagger).
4. **Corner pop with scale-settle**: the eye.
   - Pupil centre f50 (10, 12) → f53 (26, 41) → f56 (62, 68).
   - Scale at f53 is about 1.3× the final (pupil 46 px vs 37 px; iris radius 83 vs 63.5 px). It lands at 1.0 at f56.
   - 3 drawings, 6 frames (0.2 s). Read as a stepped spring: `spring({damping: 14, stiffness: 180, mass: 1})` sampled every 3 frames, applied to translate, and scale from 1.3 to 1.
   - Hold: 12 frames (f56–f67), position exactly static (0 px drift).
5. **Retreat exit (reverse of entry)**: the eye.
   - f68 (34, 52), f71 (9, 34), f74 partial (about −15, 22), f78 gone.
   - 4 drawings, 10 frames (0.33 s), linear, about 30 px per step (7%H). It leaves along the same diagonal it came in on.
6. **Flap-hop on 2s**: the bird.
   - Centroid f59 (8, 114) and f61 (8, 115): same place, new wing pose. Then f63 (64, 107) and f65 (140, 48), and it vanishes at f68 without leaving frame.
   - Pose changes every **2 frames** (15 fps), with jumps of 56 px and then 97 px up-right (accelerating).
   - Size about 41×17 px (5.6%W).
7. **Label drop (stamp-land)**: TRUST.
   - Offset f80 −95 px (−23%H, partly off the top), f83 −50 px (−12%H), f86 0. So 45 px then 50 px: linear to slightly accelerating, hard land.
   - No bounce, no scale punch. 3 drawings, 6 frames (0.2 s).
8. **Hand-grab and drag-out**: the hand removes the note.
   - The hand **appears in place** at f101 with no entry; the paper has held since f98, so 1 drawing of grip.
   - Then hand and paper move down together. Hand top (knuckles): f101 <200, f104 241, f108 321, f110 372, f113 gone. Steps of about 66, 80 and 51 px (16–19%H per drawing).
   - 4 drawings, 12 frames (0.4 s). Linear. The prop exits through the edge it came in from.
9. **Writing hand (puppet-hand)**: the brush hand.
   - Rises from the bottom-left: f116 hand top about 345, f119 about 240, f123 about 170 (brush tip at y about 90). That is 3 drawings, about 85 px per step.
   - **Animates on 2s** while "writing" (f119, f121, f123, f125). The hand position jitters ±15–35 px between those drawings and there is small rotation.
   - The text pops on per phrase at f123 and f125.
   - It sinks back out f128 → f131 → f134 → gone at f138 (4 drawings, about 0.33 s).
10. **Walk-in-place (rotoscoped live action)**: the figure.
    - A real-footage woman walking towards the camera. The pose changes on every drawing (box diff 2–8), the footprint stays anchored at (335, 278) ±4 px for the full 5.4 s, and the height is about 55 px = 13%H.
    - Her dark-pixel area swings from 280 to 900 px as the coat opens and closes. It is not scaling.
    - It survives the greyscale → colour slide-over (f11 → f14) as a separate top layer.
11. **Jaw iris close (finale)**.
    - Upper teeth drop from the top: incisal edge y 20 (f140) → 71 (f143) → 144 (f146), accelerating (51, 73 px).
    - f146 swaps in a full open-mouth photo whose dark interior is replaced by the collage.
    - The jaw then closes in steps: the lower incisal edge goes about 325 (f146) → 250 (f153) → 226 (f158) while the upper stays at about 176–186. The visible collage gap goes from about 180 px to 40 px (43%H to 10%H).
    - f161 hard-swaps to a closed-bite photo.
    - In total 7 drawings, 21 frames (0.7 s).

**Cadence (precise).** Drawing changes happen at f5, 8, 11, 14, **18**, 20, 23, 26, 29, **33**, 35, 38, 41, 44, **48**, 50… The intervals repeat **3-3-3-4-2** (15 frames = 5 drawings = exactly 10 fps on average). Every 5th drawing slips 1 frame late against a perfect 3-frame grid.

Exceptions:
- f56→f61: a 5-frame hold for the eye.
- f59–f65 and f119–f125: runs of 2-frame intervals. Only the bird and the writing hand animate there, at 15 fps.

The run-length histogram (3: 29 runs, 2: 10, 4: 8) matches this pattern.

**Boil / jitter: none.** Static layers keep 0 px displacement across every drawing; the best-fit shift is (0,0) on the rocks, the TRUST label and the red paper between f48, f56, f89 and f134. Pixel MAD between holds is 0.0–0.1, apart from encoder refreshes of about 2.5 at f50, f80, f104 and f121, with an unchanged mean. The only things that "live" while held are the figure's walk cycle and the 2s animation of the bird and brush hand. The stop-motion feel comes from the stepped timing alone, not from wobble.

## 10. Camera
No camera moves at all: no pan, zoom, shake or parallax. Global phase correlation on static regions gives 0 px across the whole piece. All motion comes from individual layers translating. The only scale change is the eye's 1.3→1.0 settle and the road's 0.96→1.0.

## 11. Code recipes (Remotion, TSX)

**a) Stepped time on the measured 10 fps grid (with the 3-3-3-4-2 slip)**
```ts
// Use the simple form for most work: const t = Math.floor(frame / 3) * 3;
// Exact source pattern (drawings at 4,7,10,13,17,19,22,... 0-based, period 15):
const PATTERN = [0, 3, 6, 9, 13]; // 0-based offsets inside each 15-frame block
export const stepped = (frame: number, offset = 4) => {
  const f = frame - offset;
  if (f < 0) return 0;
  const block = Math.floor(f / 15) * 15;
  const r = f - block;
  const k = PATTERN.filter((p) => p <= r).pop()!;
  return block + k + offset; // frame number of the drawing currently held
};
export const on2s = (frame: number) => Math.floor(frame / 2) * 2; // for the bird / writing hand
```

**b) Slide-over and rise-in on the grid (linear vs ease-out, hard stop)**
```ts
import {interpolate, Easing} from 'remotion';
const s = stepped(frame);
// Full-frame landscape covers the B&W photo: 5 steps of ~17%H, linear, done at f19 (0-based)
const coverY = interpolate(s, [4, 19], [-81, 0], {extrapolateLeft: 'clamp', extrapolateRight: 'clamp'});
const coverStyle = {transform: `translateY(${s < 4 ? -100 : coverY}%)`};
// Note paper rises with ease-out over 4 drawings (31%H → 0)
const noteY = interpolate(s, [85, 97], [50, 0], {
  easing: Easing.bezier(0.2, 0.6, 0.35, 1), extrapolateLeft: 'clamp', extrapolateRight: 'clamp',
});
```

**c) Corner pop with stepped scale-settle (the eye)**
```ts
import {spring} from 'remotion';
const local = stepped(frame) - 49; // eye starts at f50 (1-based)
const p = spring({frame: Math.max(0, local), fps: 30, config: {damping: 14, stiffness: 180, mass: 1}});
const x = interpolate(p, [0, 1], [-60, 0]);       // px at 736-wide; scale ×2.61 for 1920
const y = interpolate(p, [0, 1], [-60, 0]);
const sc = interpolate(local, [0, 3, 6], [1.3, 1.3, 1.0], {extrapolateRight: 'clamp'});
// exit: reverse along the same diagonal over 4 drawings, linear
```

## 12. Signature details and anti-patterns
**Must have**
- Stepped timing at about 10 fps, so positions change only every 3 frames, with ≤ 6 drawings per move and hard stops.
- No idle wobble. Held layers must be pixel-still.
- One live-action, rotoscoped figure anchored in place (about 13%H tall), walking for the whole piece.
- A restrained olive, khaki and cream palette with **one** blood-red paper (`#9e2522`), and naturalistic photos chosen for that range.
- Torn papers inserted behind a photo silhouette (depth slots), entering from frame corners.
- Real photographed props (eye, hands, teeth) used as puppets. They enter and exit through the nearest edge, one prop at a time, in beats of about 0.5–0.8 s.
- Ransom-style cut newsprint word: serif bold caps, split into 2 rotated torn pieces.
- An ending that turns the collage into the inside of something (mouth matte).

**Anti-patterns**
- Smooth 30/60 fps tweening, springy overshoot on translations, or squash-and-stretch.
- Global boil, film grain flicker, camera shake or parallax drift (none here).
- Drop shadows, white outlines or sticker borders around cut-outs. This piece is flat, with no shadows.
- Several saturated accents, neon, or pure white backgrounds.
- Cross-dissolves and fades. Every change is a cover, a slide or a hard swap.
- Typed or per-letter text animation. Text arrives as whole physical pieces (a dropped label, phrase-by-phrase handwriting).

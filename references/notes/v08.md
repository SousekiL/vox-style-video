# v08: travel postcard collage (vertical)

Source: `references/videos/v08_768004542745339452.mp4`. Work files: `references/analysis/v08/work/` (scripts `montage.py`, `griddiff.py`, `events.py`, `yellow.py`, `rot.py`, `track.py`, `cardpose.py`, `ink.py`, plus native extracts in `work/native/`).
Coordinates: % of frame width (W) or height (H). Frame N is at t = (N-1)/30 s. In measurements, "px@304" means pixels in the 304×540 analysis frames (1 px@304 = 0.33 %W = 0.185 %H). "Native" means 720×1280.

## 1. Summary
A single 16.4 s vertical shot of a travel postcard. One collage plate never moves: a teal grunge-textured sky with sepia clouds, a Greek ruin cut-out, a sepia Venice photo, and a live-action sea strip along the bottom. In the first 1.5 s about nine cut-outs appear in a staggered build. Each one does not slide in smoothly. It jumps between 2–3 held poses spaced 5–7 frames apart, like stop-motion. A tilted blank postcard lands in the middle of the frame. A handwritten message then writes itself one character every 2 frames for about 12.5 s, and a red "made with love" rubber stamp springs on at the end. The look comes from three things working together: a locked, dense collage; small looping secondary motion on every prop (sun and globe spin, the car wobbles, the earrings turn, the asterisks pulse on 5s); and the slow handwriting that drives the piece's whole timing.

## 6. Layout and composition
- **Centre anchor**: the postcard is centred at about (50 %W, 50 %H). It is ≈ 82 %W wide and ≈ 29 %H tall (aspect ≈ 1.6), rotated **−10°** (right side up). Final top-left corner is at (5.6 %W, 40 %H).
- **Corner satellites**: small props pin the card's corners and break its outline:
  - top-right asterisk at (85.8 %W, 29.4 %H), overlapping the top-right corner
  - lower-left asterisk at (12.9 %W, 69.8 %H), overlapping the bottom-left corner
  - car at (83.2 %W, 65.2 %H), overlapping the bottom-right corner
  - earrings below the card at (62.8 %W, 82.4 %H)
- **Top arc**: sun (40 %W, 13.3 %H, Ø ≈ 19 %W), plane (71.5 %W, 12 %H, ≈ 29 %W long) and globe (86.8 %W, 17.6 %H, Ø ≈ 14 %W) form a diagonal rising to the right, above the ruin (0–48 %W, 10–39 %H).
- **Horizontal bands of the base plate**: sky 0–42 %H, Venice photo 42–83 %H, sea 82–100 %H.
- **Depth (back to front)**, 9 layers: sky + clouds plate → sun → ruin → Venice photo → sea → plane, globe → postcard (+ handwriting, + stamp) → asterisks → car → earrings. The sun sits *behind* the ruin's entablature. The asterisks and car sit *on top of* the card.
- Empty space is essentially 0 %. Full-bleed density; the teal sky is the only breathing area. No margins, frames or grids. The card itself is the framing device.

## 8. Motion vocabulary
1. **Hold-jump entry (posed placement)**. An element appears in a first pose, often partly off-frame or in a "wrong" spot. It holds there completely still apart from its own loop, then cuts with **no in-between frames** to its final pose. Holds are 5–7 frames (0.17–0.23 s). Measured examples:
   - Sun: pose 1 at (15.5 %W, 6.7 %H), held f9–f15 (7 frames). On f16 it jumps (+24.4 %W, +6.6 %H) to its final spot. Same size (52 px@304, 17 %W).
   - Postcard: 3 poses on 5s, same scale throughout ("POSTCARD" title 160 px wide in all three).
     - f18–22: centre offset (+44 %W, −31 %H) from final, top-left corner (50 %W, 9 %H), rotation ≈ −7.5°, mostly off-frame right.
     - f23–27: offset (+29 %W, −19 %H), rotation ≈ −6.5°.
     - f28: final, −10°.
     - The step sizes grow (15 %W/12 %H, then 29 %W/19 %H). It reads as a posterized ease-in slide that snaps on without ease-out.
   - Car: pose 1 at (93 %W, 88 %H), half off-frame bottom-right, held f33–39 (7 f). Final from f40, a jump of (−10 %W, −23 %H).
   - Lower-left asterisk: pose 1 at (2 %W, 91 %H), half off-frame, f36–42 (7 f). Final from f43 (12.9 %W, 69.8 %H).
   - Earrings: pose 1 at (86 %W, 95 %H), half off-frame, f39–45 (7 f). Final from f46 (62.8 %W, 82.4 %H).
   - Globe: right edge (~97 %W, 10 %H) at f15 → intermediate at f23 → final at f28.
   - Plane:
     - f17: a tiny far-away version (~15 % scale) near its final spot, visible for 1 frame. The postcard covers that area f18–22.
     - f23–32: full size, near-final (NCC 0.82–0.89 against the final).
     - f33: one overexposed/white **flash frame**.
     - f34: final.
   - Stagger: element starts f9, 15, 17, 18, 25, 33, 36, 39. Mean gap ≈ 4 frames; the whole build takes 38 frames (1.27 s).
   - Easing: stepped, with no ease. The final pose is hit exactly; no overshoot on any placed element.
2. **Asterisk pulse on 5s (6 fps scale boil)**. Both asterisks cycle size big → mid → small → mid. Each state is held exactly 5 frames, so the period is 20 frames (0.667 s, 1.5 Hz).
   - Top-right sizes: 50 / 40 / 35 / 40 px@304 = 16.4 / 13.2 / 11.5 / 13.2 %W. Scale relative to big: 1.00 / 0.79 / 0.70 / 0.79.
   - Lower-left: 45 / 35 / 32 / 35 px (1.00 / 0.78 / 0.71 / 0.78).
   - In phase. "Big" falls on f41–45, 61–65, 81–85 … (f ≡ 1–5 mod 20). Verified f25–f493 in `yellow_all.txt`.
   - Entry: the top-right asterisk pops in at "big" for 1 frame (f25), then joins the cycle at "mid".
   - Centre fixed (±0.3 px), no rotation.
3. **Slow spin**. Smooth 30 fps clockwise in-plane rotation, continuous for the whole piece.
   - Sun: 0.60°/frame = **18°/s**. Measured f20→f32: −7°; f20→f120 ≈ −60°. It has 12 rays (≈ 30° symmetry).
   - Globe (2D disk rotation, not a 3D spin): 0.55°/frame ≈ 16.5°/s (f40→f80 −22°, f40→f120 −40°).
   - Linear, no easing.
4. **Car wobble**. Smooth, irregular low-frequency rocking.
   - Rotation 0…+8° relative to the f60 pose (mean ≈ +4°). Translation ±2.5 px@304 = ±0.8 %W, ±0.5 %H.
   - Quasi-periods 40–60 frames (~0.6 Hz), with no repeat across the 16 s. It reads like an AE `wiggle(0.6, 4)`.
   - Measured every 3 frames f40–f160.
5. **Earring turntable**. The earrings are a looping product cut-out turning about a vertical axis. The stone face shows and hides; the template correlation drops from 0.95 to 0.45 within about 60 frames. The pair's centre drifts 185–191 px@304 (2 %W). Smooth, 30 fps.
6. **Stamp scale-punch (spring)**. f468: absent → scale 0.78 (f469) → 1.26 (f470) → 1.15 (f471) → 0.95 (f472) → 0.92 (f473) → 0.97 (f474) → 1.00 (f475, settled).
   - Overshoot +26 %, undershoot −8 %, settles in 6 frames (0.2 s). Scale is from the red-ink area, √(area/area_final).
   - Position is fixed (centre native (511, 503) = 71 %W, 39.3 %H). It lands exactly on the frame the signature finishes (f469).
   - Fitted spring (simulated: 0, 0.62, 1.25, 1.22, 0.98, 0.91, 0.98, 1.03): `{mass: 0.2, stiffness: 350, damping: 6}` (≡ mass 1, stiffness 1750, damping 30).
7. **Handwriting write-on**. 1 character per 2 frames (15 char/s), spaces included, for f56 to about f421: ~190 characters in ~12.2 s. Glyphs appear whole. Ink area grows linearly (13 → 629 px@304 between f60 and f420, sampled in matching asterisk phases).
8. **Signature write-on**. 1 character per 4 frames (7.5 char/s), with an 8-frame pause after the comma (f425–f469).
9. **Live sea**. Continuous live-action footage, not a loop. The water band never repeats; minimum diff to f60 is at f61 (8.5) and rises to over 24 after 5 frames. Per-frame diff in the band ≈ 6–9/255.
10. **Flash frame**. The plane goes white/overexposed for a single frame (f33) between its pre-final and final pose. A one-frame "glint".

## 12. Signature details and anti-patterns
Must have:
- A locked camera and a fully static base collage. All motion is local and small.
- Entries as 2–3 held poses with hard cuts (5–7 frame holds, the first pose half off-frame), staggered about 4 frames apart. Never a smooth eased slide.
- Several *concurrent* secondary loops at different cadences: smooth 30 fps spins/wobble/sea, 15 fps handwriting, and a 6 fps size pulse on the yellow asterisks. This mix of rates is the texture of the piece.
- Handwriting that reveals glyph by glyph (not a stroke draw, not a fade), centre-aligned, in warm sepia ink, rotated with the card.
- Teal/cream two-tone grading, with saturated yellow as the only accent, and red held back for the final stamp.
- White sticker borders on "object" cut-outs (plane, car) only. Architecture is cut cleanly.
- The payoff spring stamp is timed to the last signature letter.

Would break it:
- Smooth tweened entries, motion blur, or any camera move, push or parallax.
- Drop shadows or 3D lighting on the card. The look is flat paste-up.
- Pure white or pure black anywhere in the collage (clouds are cream, ink is sepia).
- Boil/jitter on static photos (v08 has none).
- Typing the message as a typewriter font, or writing it in under 5 s. The slow 15 char/s pace carries the runtime.
- Adding more than one saturated accent before the payoff.
